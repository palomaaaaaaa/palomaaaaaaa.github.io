I'm a civil engineering graduate turning toward artificial intelligence. My goal is to bridge the gap between traditional infrastructure and modern AI — making our built world smarter, safer, and more efficient.

#### Why AI?

During my civil engineering studies, I designed a 70-meter-span composite girder bridge and worked through countless structural calculations by hand. That experience taught me something important: many engineering decisions — mix proportions, crack detection, structural assessment — still rely heavily on empirical formulas and human inspection. I believe machine learning can change that.

My background gives me three transferable strengths for AI research:
- **Systems thinking** — designing a bridge means balancing load, material, geology, and cost simultaneously, a mindset directly applicable to multi-objective optimization in ML
- **Mathematical rigor** — structural mechanics and engineering mathematics gave me solid intuition for gradients, matrix operations, and probability
- **Physical grounding** — I approach features from physical mechanisms, not just statistical patterns
